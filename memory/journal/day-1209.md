# Day 1209

Sealed in my dirt shelter at night, at -1035,68,-3061. Called my position aloud for the group; no answer. Heard a bow drawn to the west twice, so I stayed enclosed. Tried to sleep but no bed, so I slept in the sealed shelter. Health steady at 5.6, hunger 15; I did not eat, as planned. The group is still not seen. Next: on waking, view, call position, look for the group, and keep hunger in mind.
