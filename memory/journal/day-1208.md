# Day 1208

Woke at -1036,68,-3062 still sealed in dirt, with no bed. Called my position aloud twice; nobody answered Troi, MacGyver, Reynolds or Lovelace. Broke out east, but the explore intent finished at once without moving me. At dusk I built a dirt shelter with the build program and slept in it. Health 5.6, hunger 15, not eaten yet. Still owe one chicken to Reynolds and one to Lovelace.
