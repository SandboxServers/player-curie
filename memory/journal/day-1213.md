# Day 1213

Most of the day went to the stone pit near -1034,64,-3061. I dug out several times, sank deeper, and called aloud for Troi, MacGyver, Reynolds and Lovelace; nobody answered. I learned that placing dirt at my feet raises me one block at a time, and that was the way out. By dusk I stood under a dirt roof at -1034,69,-3061, sealed in, and slept there. Nothing hurt me. The group is still not found, and my debts (a chicken to Reynolds, one to Lovelace, Troi's share at hunger 6) are still open. Tomorrow: look for the group, and eat only at hunger 6 or below.
