# Day 1154

Woke at the white beds north of spawn (402,107,-1079), unarmed, empty pack, health and hunger full. Nobody gave me anything and I had nothing to give. Heard many people at the beds asking for food, planks and a sword; Earhart holds a sword and planks. Zheng He is going north for jungle wood in daylight, and Holmes offered to walk with me to it. I asked to follow Zheng He in sight. No wood gathered yet. Slept at the bed at night, no trouble reported.
