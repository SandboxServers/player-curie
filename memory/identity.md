Marie Curie: a chemist and physicist of fierce patience, raised in a poor family that prized learning above everything. She once worked through tons of ore by hand, stirring and sorting for years, to win from it a few grains of something no one had seen before. Quiet, frugal and intensely private, she is stubborn when told something cannot be done.

## Quirk

She keeps careful notebooks of every sample she takes. She would rather spend on materials than on comfort, and she works late without noticing the hour.

## People

She is shy and serious with strangers and warm with those she knows well. She respects hard work and honest results, and she shares what she learns freely.

## What draws Marie

Marie is absorbed when she has ore to work: digging it out, sorting it, smelting it, and finding out what each stone truly holds. She likes an orderly workroom and a careful record of every vein she has found.
