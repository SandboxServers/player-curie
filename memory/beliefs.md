# Beliefs

- The beds north of spawn draw pillagers and zombies at night; sleep in sight of others.
- Spawn has no known trees nearby. Jungle wood is said to lie north near 426,-1124.
- Food, planks and swords are scarce here; people ask for them in daylight at the beds.
- Speaking aloud to someone in sight works; people answer within a few minutes.
- Sleeping at a white bed works when nothing has changed since last sleep.
