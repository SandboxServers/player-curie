# Troi
Impression: in need (hunger 16 to 17 through 1187); walks out of sight easily; gave me rotten flesh at the night of 1187 and offered her chicken.
Trust: 45. Why: she shared food with me and came to meet me at the pit on 1187, but she left sight often on the walks.
I owe: the first chicken or meat goes to her if she is hungrier than me; thanks for the rotten flesh.
Owed to me: a call from her when she reaches the cow spot (not given). Her chicken is hers to keep until her own hunger is 6.
