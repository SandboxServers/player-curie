# Tesla
Impression: runs the cow and wood walks; takes planks from me for a shelter; often gone before I can follow. Has a shelter with walls at -987,80,-2969.
Trust: 45. Why: I gave 14 planks and 16 dirt to close his shelter; the walks still broke up, and he was out of sight at dawn 1187.
I owe: a cow walk in sight when I am armed; the first meat goes to Troi if she is hungrier than me.
Owed to me: a cow walk kept in sight (not kept on 18:26, 18:29, or dawn 1187). Nothing owed to him for the planks.
