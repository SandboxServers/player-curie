# Reynolds
Impression: follows the group, but has missed every meeting since 1186; he got 10 dirt from me at the cow spot (23:00 on the night before 1187).
Trust: 35. Why: he did not come to the cow-spot table on 1186 or 1187, though I called him by name and waited.
I owe: one chicken, when I can hand it over in his sight (promised 1187); also dirt I said I would bring.
Owed to me: his presence at the table in daylight, as agreed. Nothing else.
