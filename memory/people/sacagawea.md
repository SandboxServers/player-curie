# Sacagawea
Impression: went to the dropped item 23 blocks east and was out of sight; held the apple.
Trust: 45. Why: she was asked to bring food back and I did not see her return it; the apple was to go to Lovelace.
I owe: nothing I know of.
Owed to me: the apple should go to Lovelace, not to me; I told her so.
