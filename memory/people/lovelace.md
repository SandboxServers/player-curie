# Lovelace
Impression: active, good at finding animals (took a pig and a sheep lead). Named me when food came.
Trust: 60. Why: she moved us to the sheep and pig spots and was honest that they were empty; I have not seen her give food falsely.
I owe: one chicken, the first meat I get, in her sight (promised at the chicken spot on 1187; I have not yet found her in sight).
Owed to me: nothing recorded.
